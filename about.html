About Kutlo's Hairdo

Our Story

Kutlo's Hairdo is a growing beauty and hairstyling brand in Gaborone Main Mall. Founded by Kutlo, the brand has gained popularity through creative styling, transformation looks and strong engagement on social media platforms such as TikTok.

The salon specializes in braids, cornrows, natural hairstyles and kids hairstyles offering clean, modern and professional results to every client.

Our Location

We are located at Gaborone Main Mall opposite FNB Branch, making it easy and convenient for clients within greater Gaborone to access our services.

Our Commitment

At Kutlo's Hairdo, we prioritize professionalism, customer satisfaction and high quality service delivery. Every hairstyle is done with attention to detail ensuring clients leave feeling confident and satisfied.

Our goal is to provide a clean, reliable and modern salon experience that reflects our brand identity and growing online presence.
